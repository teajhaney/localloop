About This Event

Join us for an intentional, hands-on Sunday afternoon at Clay By The Bay in Nob Hill. Whether you have never touched a potter’s wheel or are refining your centering and trimming, this community open studio provides a relaxed, unhurried environment to practice clay craft.

Resident ceramicists will provide group demonstrations on wheel centering, opening, pulling cylinders, and dipping finished bisque pieces in studio glazes. After the demo, participants have open access to wheels and hand-building workbenches with one-on-one guidance available throughout the session.
